### Market problem

Local service businesses waste budget on generic agencies that optimize for impressions. They need measurable leads and channel expertise without building an in-house growth team.

### State of the art

| Model | Gap |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional local agencies | Assumptions over measurement |
| Pure self-serve ad tools | No operator leverage |
| Generic national agencies | Weak vertical depth |

**BrandBooster’s wedge:** data-first lead gen for local verticals with AI accelerating creative/ops.

### Approach

- Co-founded and shaped GTM for local-services lead generation  
- Combined PPC expertise with AI-assisted production  
- Positioned outcomes (leads, ROAS) over activity metrics
