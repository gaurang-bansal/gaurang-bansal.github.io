### Market problem

Local service businesses bleed budget on generic agencies and DIY ads. They need predictable lead volume without hiring a growth team. BrandBooster’s public wedge: **vertical playbooks + AI-assisted creative + human media ops**.

### State of the art

| Model | Gap for contractors |
| --- | --- |
| National generic agencies | Weak local/job-type nuance |
| Self-serve ads | No creative/ops leverage |
| Lead marketplaces | Shared, low-intent leads |
