### Market problem

Ad spy tools stop at inspiration. Agencies are slow. Pure AI image tools are fast but off-brand. Performance teams needed one loop: discover → create → publish → manage.

### State of the art

QuickAds targeted closing that loop with a proprietary creative-intelligence layer **plus** human strategists who had managed large ad spend — platform and service, not tool-only.

### Approach

- Co-founded company; drove product/engineering for AI image &amp; video  
- Shipped Discover, brand kits from URL, multi-format export  
- Combined LLM APIs with operator workflows for brand-safe output
