Co-founded [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/), an AI advertising SaaS that helps marketers create image ads, video ads, product photography, and virtual fashion creatives. The product includes a Discover ad library and automation workflows used by D2C teams. QuickAds launched publicly in 2024, scaled through AppSumo, and received a Stevie Award for AI innovation.

**Stack:** FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Next.js, React, AWS, OpenAI APIs
