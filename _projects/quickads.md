### Market problem we attacked

Creative is the bottleneck in performance marketing: agencies are slow/expensive; spy tools stop at inspiration; raw AI image tools are fast but off-brand. QuickAds’ bet was a **single OS** — intelligence + generation + launch — so D2C and growth teams stop guessing.

### State of the art (then)

| Approach | Limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional agencies | Weeks of shoots/approvals; poor test volume |
| Ad spy tools | Inspiration without production |
| DIY GenAI | Speed without competitive grounding or publish ops |

### My contribution (ownership)

- Co-founded the company; owned critical **product + engineering** surfaces for AI image/video generation and the Discover intelligence layer  
- Helped ship brand kits, multi-format export, and campaign-oriented workflows  
- Operated in a VC-backed environment with AppSumo launch momentum and award recognition  

### Approach

World-class operators + AI pipelines + proprietary ad signals as **one system** — briefs informed by what wins, creatives generated for conversion, then measured and iterated.
