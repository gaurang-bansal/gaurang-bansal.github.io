Previously **co-founded [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/)** (through August 2025) — an AI advertising SaaS that turns a product URL or brief into **market-tested image and video ads** for D2C brands and agencies.

### Positioning

Where legacy “ad spy” tools stop at inspiration, QuickAds closed the loop: **discover → analyze → generate → publish**. The Discover library spans Meta, YouTube, TikTok and more (**30M+** creatives), with AI scoring for hooks, clarity, and brand fit — then one-click image/video generation and multi-format export.

### What shipped

- AI **image & video** ad generation (scripts, storyboards, voiceovers, product photography / virtual fashion)
- Multi-platform **Discover** library with competitive teardown / SWOT-style insights
- Brand kits from URL, 30+ formats, publishing workflows to major ad channels
- Scaled usage reported in the tens of thousands of brands and millions of creatives generated

### Outcomes

- Standout **AppSumo** launch (2024)
- **Stevie Award** for AI / technology innovation
- Backed on a **Series A–track / VC-funded** growth path (Tier-1 investors including Kae Capital)
