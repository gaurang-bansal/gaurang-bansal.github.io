### State of the art (and the gap)

Legacy “ad spy” tools stop at inspiration. Traditional agencies are slow and expensive. Pure AI image tools are fast but often off-brand and still need heavy finishing.

**What QuickAds targeted:** close the loop — **discover → create → optimize &amp; publish → campaign management** — with proprietary creative intelligence plus human strategists.

### My contribution

- Co-founded the company and drove product/engineering for AI image &amp; video generation
- Helped ship Discover (multi-platform ad intelligence), brand kits from URL, and multi-format export
- Built on FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Next.js/React, AWS, and LLM APIs
- Supported AppSumo launch momentum and recognition (Stevie Award; backed by Kae Capital and leaders from Google, Shopify, and Meta)

### What shipped

| Loop | Capability |
| --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Search 30M+ high-performing ads across Meta, TikTok, Google, and more |
| **Create** | Image ads, video ads, UGC, AI product &amp; virtual fashion photoshoots |
| **Optimize &amp; publish** | Pre-test, brand compliance, 1-click deploy to YouTube, TikTok, Meta, Google |
| **Campaign management** | Full-funnel strategy and ROAS ops across Meta, Google, Amazon, TikTok |

### Outcomes

- **30K+ brands**, **120+ countries**, **500K+ ads generated** (product-reported)
- Company-reported early lifts include stronger CTR, lower CPM, and ROAS within the first month
- Stevie Award for AI / technology innovation; AppSumo breakout launch
- Left **August 2025** to build my own ventures

<p class="case-note">Impact metrics above are published by QuickAds (site / LinkedIn) and reflect product-reported aggregates.</p>
