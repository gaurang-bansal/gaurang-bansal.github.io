<div class="research-block">
  <strong>System</strong>
  A deep-learning adversary / analysis pipeline against anonymity layers in IoT routing — from traffic/features to deanonymization hypotheses that stress-test privacy claims.
</div>

<div class="research-block">
  <strong>Problem</strong>
  IoT anonymity protocols are often evaluated against classical attackers. Neural adversaries can exploit structure classical analyses miss, creating a false sense of privacy.
</div>

<div class="research-block">
  <strong>Our contribution</strong>
  Applied deep models to anonymity analysis in IoT routing; articulated security takeaways for privacy-preserving network design; published the attack/analysis framing for the community.
</div>

### Achievements

- Peer-reviewed deep-learning analysis of anonymous IoT routing  
- Actionable guidance: design anonymity assuming neural adversaries, not only classic ones
