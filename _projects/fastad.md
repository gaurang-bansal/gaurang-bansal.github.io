### Market problem

Attention windows close in days. Agencies are cinematic but late. Pure DIY AI is fast but trend-blind and uneven on brand. Fast Ad’s public thesis: **real-time ad engine** — sense → produce → publish.

### State of the art

| Option | Gap |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional agency | Slow; weak trend scrapers |
| DIY GenAI tools | No distribution timing |
| Spy tools | Inspiration without production |
