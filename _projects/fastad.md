### State of the art (and the gap)

Short-form video wins attention, but most brands still wait on slow agency cycles or ship generic AI clips that miss trend timing and brand fit. Pure DIY tools are fast but uneven; traditional production is high-quality but too slow for trend windows.

**What Fast Ad advances:** trend-aware creative production — detect what is moving in your category, then ship platform-optimized video ads and social content in days, not weeks, with AI speed and human creative judgment.

### My contribution

- Founded the company and product direction for AI + human video marketing
- Positioned Fast Ad around industry trend detection and ~48-hour ready-to-publish delivery
- Built the narrative and go-to-market for scroll-era video ads (Instagram, YouTube, Meta) and social content (reels, carousels, branded creatives)
