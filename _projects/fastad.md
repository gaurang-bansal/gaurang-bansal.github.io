### Market problem

Attention moves in hours, not weeks. Traditional agencies are high quality but slow. Pure DIY AI video is fast but often off-brand and trend-blind. Brands lose the scroll window waiting on either extreme.

### State of the art

| Approach | Strength | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agencies | Craft, strategy | Days–weeks of latency |
| DIY AI tools | Speed | Weak trend timing & brand consistency |
| Spy + manual edit | Inspiration | Still human bottleneck |

**Fast Ad’s bet:** treat trend detection + rapid production as one product — not two disconnected vendors.

### Approach

1. Sense category / competitor motion early  
2. Brief creatives against that motion  
3. Generate and refine with AI + human editors  
4. Deliver platform-ready video and social packs in ~48 hours
