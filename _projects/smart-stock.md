<div class="research-block">
  <strong>System</strong>
  A decentralized exchange architecture coupled with RNN-based predictors over ledger transaction streams — forecasting and settlement assumptions co-designed rather than bolted on.
</div>

<div class="research-block">
  <strong>Problem</strong>
  Decentralized markets need both integrity of settlement and intelligent prediction. Treating blockchain as dumb storage and ML as an offline add-on leaves a gap between security guarantees and predictive utility.
</div>

<div class="research-block">
  <strong>Our contribution</strong>
  Formulated a secure predictive decentralized market model; applied recurrent learning to ledger-backed transaction dynamics; connected ML forecasting with distributed settlement constraints.
</div>

### Achievements

- Peer-reviewed publication on smart / predictive stock exchange designs  
- Concrete bridge between RNN forecasting and decentralized market rails
