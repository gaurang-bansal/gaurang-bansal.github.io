**Research Collaborator with [Google](https://about.google/)** (alongside [Sai Teja Peddinti](https://sites.google.com/site/psaiteja/home) and NUS collaborators) on automated phishing detection.

### Problem

Phishing sites mutate faster than manual blocklists. Production defenses need **high precision**, low annotation cost, and signals that generalize to brand-new campaigns.

### Approach

- Domain-specific signals and **logo / visual similarity** comparison
- Deep learning with unsupervised and one-shot style identification
- Paths toward **browser-plugin** deployment for real-time warnings

### Impact

Research that bridges academic methods and productizable browser security — reducing reliance on brittle, manually curated training sets while keeping false positives in check.
