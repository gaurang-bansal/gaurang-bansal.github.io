What the product offers

Discover Search a huge multi-platform ad library (Meta, TikTok, Google, and more) to see what is winning in a niche — proprietary creative intelligence, not moodboards alone.
Create AI image & video ads, UGC-style content, catalogue creatives, virtual fashion / product photoshoots — on-brand variants at testing speed.
Optimize & publish Pre-test creative, brand checks, and 1-click deploy paths toward YouTube, TikTok, Meta, and Google.
Campaign management Full-funnel strategy and ROAS ops across major channels — platform + operators, not tool-only.

Market problem we attacked

Creative is the bottleneck in performance marketing: agencies are slow/expensive; spy tools stop at inspiration; raw AI image tools are fast but off-brand. QuickAds’ bet was a single OS — intelligence + generation + launch — so D2C and growth teams stop guessing.
