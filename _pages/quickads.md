**[QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/)** is an AI-powered advertising platform that Gaurang previously co-founded (through August 2025), built to make high-quality image and video ad creation fast and accessible for D2C brands and growth teams.

### What it does

- **AI creative generation** — Instant image ads, video ads, virtual fashion photoshoots, and product photography workflows
- **Discover library** — A large ad library spanning Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Google, and TikTok creatives for D2C inspiration
- **Campaign tooling** — Targeting, iteration, and analytics support so teams can move from brief to live creative quickly

### Recognition

QuickAds launched on AppSumo in 2024, became a standout launch there, and later received a **Stevie Award for AI innovation**.
