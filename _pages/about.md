**Founder of [AdScrape](https://adscrape.in/)** — Meta Ad Library intelligence for Facebook & Instagram research (dashboard + API).

**Computer Science Expert at [Turing](https://www.turing.com/)**, working with **[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/)** on **Claude Opus** models (Opus 4.5, 4.6, through 4.8 / Fable). Focus: finding where frontier models fail—and making them better—across data science, machine learning, research-paper workflows, and multimedia.

Previously **co-founded [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/)** (AI image/video ad generation; Stevie Award for AI innovation) and served as Director at Vedastu Applications Pvt. Ltd. Left both in August 2025.

Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [NUS](https://nus.edu.sg) (2020–2025), with the [Google PhD Fellowship](https://research.google/outreach/phd-fellowship/recipients) and [NUS President Fellowship](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/ece/graduate/scholarship-financial-support). M.E. and B.E. in Computer Science from [BITS Pilani](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in) (2020, 2018).

**34+ publications**, **2,200+ citations** ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=A-t6Ds8AAAAJ&hl=en)) in machine learning, cryptography, privacy, and web security. Stack: Python (FastAPI), PostgreSQL, Next.js, React, AI/ML.

Earlier: Research Collaborator at [Google](https://about.google/) (phishing detection with [Sai Teja Peddinti](https://sites.google.com/site/psaiteja/home)); Editor, ACM XRDS Magazine; engineering at [DRIFE](https://www.drife.io).
