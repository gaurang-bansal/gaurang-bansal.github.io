Gaurang Bansal

AdScrape • PhD, NUS • Google PhD Fellow

Gaurang Bansal is an entrepreneur and AI technologist building products at the intersection of Generative AI, machine learning, and deep analytics. He is the Founder of AdScrape, a Meta Ad Library intelligence platform and API for searching, monitoring, and automating Facebook & Instagram ad research. He also works as a Computer Science Expert at Turing, contributing to large-scale AI evaluation and data systems.

He previously co-founded QuickAds, an AI-powered platform that helps brands generate image and video ads at scale—spanning creatives, product photography, and campaign workflows. QuickAds grew into a funded SaaS product used by D2C marketers worldwide and was recognized with a Stevie Award for AI innovation. He also served as Director at Vedastu Applications Pvt. Ltd. He left both QuickAds and Vedastu in August 2025.

Gaurang earned his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National University of Singapore (NUS) (2020–2025), where he was awarded the prestigious Google PhD Fellowship and the NUS President Fellowship. He completed his Master’s (2020) and Bachelor’s (2018) degrees at BITS Pilani.

His research in machine learning, cryptography, privacy, and web security has led to 30+ publications in venues such as IEEE IoT Journal and IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, with 1,000+ citations on Google Scholar. He brings 8+ years of hands-on experience across Python (FastAPI), PostgreSQL, Next.js, React, and AI/ML systems.

Earlier in his career, he was a Research Collaborator with Google, working with Sai Teja Peddinti on automated phishing detection, and engineered decentralized mobility systems at DRIFE.

Gaurang’s work combines research depth with product execution—turning advances in AI and advertising intelligence into tools that teams can ship and scale.
